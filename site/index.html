What does “prime” mean?

A prime number has exactly two positive divisors: 1 and itself. Trial division checks whether any integer from 2 up to √n divides n evenly.

Why primes matter for RSA (1 minute)

RSA key generation picks two large primes p and q, multiplies them to get n = p·q, and builds keys using modular arithmetic. Multiplication is easy, but factoring n back into p and q is hard when primes are large.
